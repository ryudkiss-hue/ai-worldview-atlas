# Thought experiments

The Atlas has 26 thought experiments: concrete situations that put the axes under pressure. They are unscored and live in [`src/data/thoughtExperiments.ts`](src/data/thoughtExperiments.ts), shown at `/thought-experiments`. They follow the plain-language rules in [`docs/LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`](docs/LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md).

1. **The Translation Engine** (Machine Experience, Moral Standing): Does Sarah understand Chinese?
2. **The Empathy Room** (Relationships, Moral Standing): Is the care real because it helps Marcus, or empty because nothing behind it cares?
3. **The Future-Self Forecast** (Human Control, Moral Standing): Should Darius follow his predicted future values, or his present ones?
4. **The Consciousness Lottery** (Machine Experience, Moral Standing): Is it better for the system to keep working without feeling, or to feel and sometimes suffer?
5. **The Delegation Trap** (Human Control, Openness & Control): If an AI system reliably produces better outcomes, should elected officials let it decide?
6. **The Scraping Dilemma** (Openness & Control, Moral Standing): Is there a difference between an artist learning from Elena and a company training a product on her work?
7. **The Secret Safeguard** (Speed and Safety, Moral Standing): Is Kenji protecting the public, or overriding it?
8. **The Paper That Argues for Itself** (Machine Experience, Moral Standing): What evidence would you need before treating Ada as something that can be harmed?
9. **The Enhancement Race** (Human Control, Openness & Control): Is enhancing human abilities a good thing if only some people can have it?
10. **The Allocation Committee** (Nations and the World, Openness & Control): Is there a right answer here, or only reasonable disagreement?
11. **The Week-Long Change** (Human Control, Relationships): Is there a fact about which values are really yours?
12. **Gratitude and Sacrifice** (Relationships, Moral Standing): Should love and appreciation depend on someone having suffered?
13. **The Historical Simulation** (Machine Experience, Moral Standing): Can someone create a being that can feel by accident?
14. **The Prediction You Didn't Ask For** (Speed and Safety, Moral Standing): Do people have a right not to know predictions about their own future?
15. **The Meaning Gap** (Machine Experience, Relationships): Is meaning something a maker puts in, something viewers find, or something that happens between them?
16. **The Questionnaire Problem** (Machine Experience, Speed and Safety): Does an assessment like this discover your views, or help form them?
17. **The One-Way Mirror** (Openness & Control, Speed and Safety): Can a surveillance system be legitimate if the people it watches can't see how it works?
18. **The Comfort Machine** (Relationships, Human Control): Is there a difference between feeling happy and doing well?
19. **The Regulator's Dilemma** (Openness & Control, Moral Standing): Can someone change a system from the inside if the system pressures them to compromise?
20. **The Dangerous Discovery** (Speed and Safety, Openness & Control): Is some knowledge too dangerous to publish?
21. **Ruth's Care Plan** (Relationships, Moral Standing): Can a machine's help and a family's care work together, or does one tend to push out the other?
22. **The Voice After Death** (Relationships, Machine Experience): Is a convincing imitation of a lost relationship a way of grieving, or a way of avoiding grief?
23. **The Linked Minds** (Human Control, Relationships): Is a big gain in what people can do together worth some loss of who they are alone?
24. **The Offset Question** (Moral Standing, Openness & Control): Can doing good in one place make up for causing harm in another?
25. **The Fifty-Year Cost** (Human Control, Moral Standing): How much should possible harm to future people weigh against certain benefits now?
26. **The Label Problem** (Machine Experience, Relationships): Can knowing your label narrow your thinking?
